Search & AI Visibility Audit

Can people — and AI — find Si?

When shoppers search Google or ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity for an affordable luxury brand in Pakistan, does Si come up? This audit checks — and lays out exactly what to fix.

Prepared for Ayesha Iftikharsi-retail.com20 November 2026
Summary

Three things stand out

Si has a strong product story — fine fabrics, honest pricing, a clear "quiet luxury" point of view. Right now, search engines and AI assistants can't see it.

0

Search results for Si's own name

In our tests, searches for "Si Retail", "si-retail.com" and "The si clothing" returned no Si pages. "SI Retail" is already used by an unrelated shop-fitting company, which takes those results.

0

Third-party mentions found

We found no press, blog, directory or review mentioning Si or its founder. AI assistants recommend brands that others talk about — so today they have nothing to cite.

5

Competitors showing up instead

For "linen co-ord sets" and "quiet luxury" searches, Limelight, Laam, The Linen Company, 1947 Clothing and Ochre Wear appear. Si does not.

AI search (AEO & GEO)

Getting Si recommended by AI assistants

"What's a good affordable luxury clothing brand in Pakistan?"

More shoppers now ask this of ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity instead of scrolling Google. These assistants recommend brands that are clearly described, consistent everywhere, and talked about by others.

Si already has

  • A distinctive story and founder
  • Detailed fabric data on every product (material, fit, stretch, feel)
  • Shopify's AI-agent files switched on
  • Clear prices and a real exchange policy

Si is missing

  • One consistent brand name and description
  • Mentions from press, blogs, creators or communities
  • Pages that answer shoppers' questions directly
  • Structured data that machines can read
The plan

90 days to being found

Ordered by impact and effort. Phase 1 alone fixes most of the website issues above.

Weeks 1–2

Fix the foundations

  • One brand name everywhere
  • Homepage title, description, heading
  • Readable product titles without SKU codes
  • Product, Organization & FAQ schema
  • Merge duplicate collections, fix typos, add alt text
Weeks 3–6

Become the answer

  • FAQ page: exchanges, sizing, fabrics, delivery
  • Si Journal: fabric & styling guides from Si's own product data
  • Richer product descriptions (English + Urdu)
  • "About Si" page written for people and AI
Weeks 6–12

Get talked about

  • Pakistani fashion press & creator outreach (disclosed)
  • Google Business Profile & directory listings
  • An official, transparent Si presence on Reddit & X
  • Founder story placements
Measuring it

How we'll know it's working

Each month we re-run the searches above, plus the same questions in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and report: does Si appear, where, and what is said about it — alongside site traffic from search and AI referrals.

Method & notes

Website checks were made on 20 November 2026 against si-retail.com's public pages, sitemap, robots.txt and Shopify product feed. Searches were run on 20 November 2026 with a web search tool based outside Pakistan, without personalisation — results seen from Pakistan, or on Google directly, may differ. "Si appears?" means no Si page or mention was among the results returned. The structured-data check covers markup present in each page's HTML. This audit describes visibility only; it makes no claims about sales or traffic, which we haven't seen.
